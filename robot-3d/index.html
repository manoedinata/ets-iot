Interactive 3D prototype · ESP32-CAM · mecanum inverse kinematics

Idle

Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · click a part · 1 unit = 10 cm

The 3D view needs WebGL and the three.js library from cdnjs. Check your connection and browser settings, then reload.

Run

Hold a key or button. Strafing sideways without turning is what the omniwheels add over a normal 4WD chassis.

The robot sweeps lanes, shifting lane by strafing. When the MQ-6 readings rise it hill-climbs toward the source using the left-minus-right gradient, then turns to look at the tank.
